Founder's Note
Why I built AVBLOODNET

My name is VIKI VADKAR, Founder & CEO of AVBLOODNET, and I am a student of Shree Sateri Pisani Educational Society's Shri Gopal Gaonkar Memorial Goa Multi-Faculty College, Dharbandora, Goa. I belong to Usgao-Tisca, Goa, and I have always believed that technology should be used to serve humanity.

Being associated with Bajrang Dal has shaped my values and vision towards society. It has taught me the importance of Seva, Suraksha, and Sanskar — selfless service, protection of society, and strong moral values. These principles inspired me to build AVBLOODNET, not just as a website, but as a mission to help people during medical emergencies when every second matters.

AVBLOODNET is a blood donor platform designed to connect voluntary blood donors with patients who urgently need blood. The donor information on the platform is collected through blood donation camps, voluntary registrations, awareness drives, and community outreach. My vision is to build a trusted and continuously growing network so that no patient has to struggle to find blood when it is needed the most.

I started this project with one simple belief: if my website helps save even one life, it will be one of the greatest achievements of my life. My dream is to expand AVBLOODNET across Goa and eventually throughout India.

"Technology has real value only when it improves people's lives. AVBLOODNET is my humble contribution towards a future where no life is lost because blood could not be found in time."
— VIKI VADKAR Founder & CEO, AVBLOODNET  |  📞 7387847771
